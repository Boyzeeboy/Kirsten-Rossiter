The Kingdom of God is not confined to church buildings. The Lord is raising up apostolic and prophetic believers who carry His presence into their homes, businesses, communities and governments, and build His Kingdom wherever He has placed them.

These posts are about that call: the apostolic and prophetic gifts working together, new models of church and leadership, Kingdom businesses, and our part in seeing His will done on earth as it is in Heaven. It is the message at the heart of my book, *[Building the Nations from the Ground Up](/building-the-nations)*.
