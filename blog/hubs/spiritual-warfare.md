There are seasons when the battle is fierce and the opposition doesn't let up. It is not only life being hard: often we are facing an enemy who is holding ground that God has already given us.

These posts are about taking that ground back: recognising the enemy's tactics, standing against territorial spirits, releasing the sound of Heaven, and praying in the Spirit for our families, our streets and our nations. The battle belongs to the Lord, and He trains our hands for war.
