Some seasons ask more of us than we thought we had to give: the desert, the valley, the long wait for a promise, the storm that doesn't seem to lift. I have walked through many of them, and in every one the Lord has proved Himself faithful.

These are the words, testimonies and encouragement He has given me for those times. They are about trusting God in hard times, finding Him in the valley, entering His rest while everything around us is shaking, and knowing the perfect love that casts out all fear.

If you are in a hard place today, I pray you find here what I have found: He is with you, and He is not finished.
