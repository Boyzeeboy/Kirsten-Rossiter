The Call of The Bride

9 July 2017

The Call of the Bride

What is the Call of The Bride at this time? What is her purpose, her vocation above all? For many would say, a building that's strong To make room for all the lost souls who would come

Now that's not a negative, unkindly sort of thing But I think it's rather a different-looking thing The Call of The Bride is to see beyond yonder Possibilities and opportunities brimming with Wonder

For the fields are golden, the grapes plump and bursting The season of picking, pressing and singing Stamping and clapping, rejoicing and feasting Has come upon her and all who surround her

The Bride is not confined to a building of stones But she lives in the field, gathering a harvest of great, fine yield She throws and she scatters to all those around her Feeding and tending the wounded besides her

She thinks not to gather as much as she can Yet her storehouses can't keep up with the intake of her grain She cares more for others than she does of herself People are drawn from the north corners, and the south

They come to her dressed in cloth streaked with dirt Their eyes staring down, their lips dry from thirst New garments she weaves, purple and scarlet She adorns them with jewels, rubies, and diamonds

She feeds them from her bountiful harvest Their ears and their eyes evidently perceives The One she resembles, the One she reflects Is the Holy One of Israel, the King of every living thing

Never did they think it possible to be So comfortable and relaxed in the Presence of this King They've never met Him, nor knew Him by His Name Even though they've heard much about Him, disregarding His fame

But here inside this banqueting hall She's served them as if they're royal and all From trivial dusty streets, they have come Yet to her, their worth is beyond any sum

The Call of The Bride is to be intertwined With her Eternal Lover, Christ, her Lord To let Him touch those in desperate need Speaking His Kind words they need to receive

To extend His Grace and share His Yield From the Harvest, He is releasing upon His Field That is her Call, that is Her Prize She's called to be His Royal, Kingly Bride
