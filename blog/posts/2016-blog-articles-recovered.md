I am so delighted to share all my old blog articles on my website again. After years of hacking, and then some technical issues that we couldn’t resolve until now, I am finally able to have them visible again.

I started writing my articles in 2016, ten years ago. However, all the articles then are so relevant today. God has been speaking through His prophets, and we are now seeing the fulfilment of what He’s been preparing us for. It is upon us!

We are in a Kairos moment where the words spoken then are connected to the appointed time, place, person, opportunity and season. We’ve entered the Kingdom Era, and everything we know is changing. That which made us secure and stable before will now be shaken. The only thing stable and secure will be Christ, our Cornerstone, and Him alone. He is teaching us to walk dynamically with Him, through turbulent changes.

God does nothing without revealing it to the prophets first. The key for us is to hear the voice of the Lord, echoing through the earth. What has He been saying over the last decade, over the last season? What are the words that confirm one another? Not the millions of voices, speaking their own opinions, fears, control, and even foretelling. The one voice that comes through those prophetic voices we trust. The humble ones who know Him. The ones who only speak when they hear and know how to hold their tongue in silence when necessary.

There are too many voices, too much noise, and too much of a muchness.

Amos 5:13 says, ‘Yes, the smartest thing for the prudent is to remain silent in such an evil time as this.’

Amos 5:14 says, ‘Devote yourself to what is good, not evil, so that you may live. And YAHWEH, the God of Angel Armies, will really be with you, just as you claim he is. Hate what is evil, love what is right, and let justice prevail in the courts. It may be that YAHWEH, God of Angel Armies, will show mercy to the remnant of Israel.’

We are in a time of judgment. God is going to restore justice on the earth, and we are going to have to find ourselves on the side of good and not evil. Righteousness and Truth.

The beauty of the prophetic is that it is given in advance to hold us up and keep us looking straight, with understanding, purpose and expectation, knowing that God is working His promises out in the midst of shaking, darkness, and calamity. It’s His reassurance that He is with us, that He knows and understands, and that He will come to our rescue as He has promised. He will accomplish His purposes in spite of what we see and feel. We can trust Him.

It would be wonderful if you could read through my prophetic blog on my website, and feel free to pop me a message to share your thoughts.

Sending you all much love and blessings!
