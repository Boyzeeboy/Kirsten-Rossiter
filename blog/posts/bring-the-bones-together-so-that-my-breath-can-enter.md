The enemy has been using distraction of every kind to keep us from focussing on what God is saying and showing. He is saying, ‘Stay in the place of My presence where I am your peace, where I am your stable ground. Do not look to the left or the right, do not be distracted, and do not open the door to fear. In My presence, I will top up your vessel of hope and faith, I will give you new strength, and I will show you, again, My promises of old that have long been forgotten. They have faded away from all the years of waiting and delay, but, My child, this is the hour to remember. It is the hour to see them again, with new eyes. 

It is the hour to activate every promise and to step into the mantle that I have prepared you for. Start walking in those promises even if your bones feel dead, and the walk is a walk without the breath in it. Prophesy to the bones as you gather them together again - it’s time to gather the bones, it’s time to bring those disjointed members together so that I can breathe into the body! It’s time to live again!

Your identity is not in the valley of the dead bones but in Me and who I have called you to be. Start walking in your calling, your identity and purpose, and see Me breathe My life into you again. I will bring you back to life, back to your dreams, back to your vision that has been dimmed and dulled over the years of the shadow.

Partner with My Spirit in this hour, My beloved. Prophesy and activate what I have called you to, and who I have called you to be in this new era upon the earth. Your new name! Step into your new name. You have been shaped into a new person through your suffering, not only in humility and brokeness, but in anointing and power! You have been sharpened, you have been hardened. An arrow in My hand. A weapon.

The earth is in travail, it is in the labour pangs, to deliver the sons and daughters of God. 

> “The entire universe is standing on tiptoe, yearning to see the unveiling of God’s glorious sons and daughters!” Rom. 8:19 (TPT)

Many will run to and fro, in search of water. Many will hunger and thirst for the presence of the Living God, for they will be in such turmoil, such pain, and such despair. I have made you into a people who will not only know how to fight the giants, but also how to comfort the hurting, how to bring deliverance to the bound, and how to usher them into the Presence of a Living God who will encounter them through His love and power. 

So, My Beloved, pick up your mantle. Sing your song again. See your vision again. Bring the bones together as you start walking in who I have destined you to be. Come out of the shadows, come out of the valley. Even, if it’s by faith only, and nothing is visible in the natural, because in that place where you see and walk, you will live again. My breath will enter your body and you will start seeing the spiritual enter your natural sphere.’
