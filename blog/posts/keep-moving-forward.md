**Vision: Crossing the Defence Circle**

The Lord showed me a picture of a circle in a circle. I heard Him say, ‘the resistance that you have been feeling, has been from crossing the outer circle band around the territory you are called to. It is the ‘fighting zone’ where the territorial giants are defending the territory that lies within. The relentless attack that My people have endured, is to keep them from moving forward into the actual territory that I have promised them, and am giving them. The enemy’s strategy is two-fold: to wear you down to the point of passivity and giving up, or to fill you with such dread that you retreat completely. However, the enemy cannot hold you back, if you keep moving forward. That is your strongest weapon. Not giving up, moving forward, and trusting Me. 

Hear My Word to you, this day: Keep moving forward! Your victory is here. You have been crossing the most dangerous terrain that has cost you everything, however, you have done well and have come to the line that separates the band of defences from the land of inheritance. Now is not the time to fear and look around, to hesitate. It is the time to advance! Now is the time to pick up your speed and gather your strength. It’s the final crossing into your promises. In this defence band, I have turned you into a warrior. Weak and tired, you may feel right now, but you are a warrior who will conquer and recover all. I will bring you into a place of establishment, peace and rest, and in this place there will be refreshment and restoration. In this place I will work the ‘effortless’ into your circumstances and life, and things will start to flow and work that were deadlocked before.’

**Joshua Instruction**

The Lord is calling us to listen to His instruction in this hour. He is giving clear instruction and fresh revelation regarding His assignment and mantle for us. He is giving us the details that we need to move forward and occupy the land on the inside. Get your pens out and write down what He is revealing. Take out your old prophetic words, dreams and vision, and scriptures and see how He is filling in the gaps and connecting the dots that you need for this era ahead.

**Vision: Conveyer Belt next to the Tsunami**

Over the last year, the Lord showed me several visions of a coming and growing Tsunami. It is a metaphor of the change that the world is set up to go through, and it’s swelling fast. One of the visions the Lord gave me a while back, was of His Bride who is prepared and ready, surfing the wave, being catapulted by it, not crushed. He showed me another vision recently, of us running next to the Tsunami on a conveyer belt. I heard the Lord say, ‘Keep moving! When you move, the conveyer belt underneath your feet, will cause you to accelerate beyond your own speed. I will increase your speed and will support and help you as you move. In this sideline, you are called to take hold of your assignment and start working it, even if it seems in the shadows. For at the right moment, when the Tsunami is at a certain height, you will jump from the belt onto the wave and ride it. That which you were doing in the shadows will become what you will do in the mainstream. That which you were faithful with in the waiting, you will be catapulted in, and be established in. I am changing the guards, and to the world, it will seem overnight. However, in My Kingdom economy, it is progressive through the preparation that happened in the shadows and sidelines. Get ready for promotion, get ready to be seen in the light, get ready to be found in the front where I am breaking new ground and establishing new systems.

**It’s all about Systems**

The Lord is saying, ‘Get ready for My apostolic era upon the earth! Everything will be established and built around systems. For I am rebuilding the nations and I am resetting the earth. My people who understand My purposes and know My will, will partner with My Spirit in the pioneering of new systems of operations for everything that the nations need. Everything will be made new. When you look at the crash, all you see is destruction. However, when you look at the wave and the surf, all you see is the ‘new’. That’s where I need your focus to be. 

Your dissatisfaction with the old systems that were designed to fail and break have been a point of disillusionment to many. However, it is the catalyst that you need to birth forth the solutions you hold within. You can’t fix a broken, corrupt system. It has to break. Then you start anew. That’s where the world is going. What you’ve experienced on a personal level, the nations will experience, and the world will experience.’

**It’s not for the Faint-Hearted**

The Lord is saying, ‘When I called out, “Who will go for Me? Who will stand up for righteousness in this evil generation?” You said, “Yes, Lord, send me”. Your ‘yes’ has cost you because you have permitted Me to prepare you. It is not for your harm but for your future and the future of the generations. The future of bloodlines and generational lines. The future of cities and nations. This is big. The enemy understands territory and bloodlines. He has claimed it and caused carnage throughout history. Everyone who has given Me their ‘Yes’ is called to advance forward in spite of the danger, the pushback, the assault, and the accusation. My strength is yours, let your reserve be found in Me when you have nothing left. I am with you, and I will deliver the enemy into your hands as you keep moving forward with your ‘Yes’ to Me and your ‘No!’ to the enemy. You are called as a Kinsman-Redeemer to your generations and bloodline, and to others’.
