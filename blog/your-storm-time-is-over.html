Your storm-time is over!

14 September 2026

Look forward and walk!

I was chatting to my husband the other day, and made a scribble on paper as I was talking—which I love to do. Being a visual learner, I often use illustrations to convey a deeper meaning by making it tangible and simple to others (and myself as I’m talking!).

I was amazed at what I drew. I was talking about the change that I’m feeling in my spirit. That we have come out of the storm. I saw waves on the left and waves on the right, and then I clearly saw a passageway in the middle running through the waves—it was called ‘Kingdom’ and referred to our calling, which I wrote down in the centre of the left and right waves. I said to him, ‘We have to look straight ahead now, just keep walking forward into what God has promised us—it is the vision before us. It is clearing a pathway between the stormy waves we’ve been walking through. Don’t look to the left or the right and be distracted, just keep walking forward.’ When I looked at my drawing at the end, I recognised that it looked like the parting of the Red Sea.

The Lord is saying the same to you today. If you’ve been going through a storm, unable to move forward, be encouraged that the Lord is calling you to keep walking forward in what He has put on your heart, even if it seems impossible. Put your hand out like Moses did, and see the Lord part the waves before you. Your storm-time is over, and the Lord is moving you forward into the passageway through the waves. It is a ‘just-keep-walking’ moment. Do not look behind; it will cause you to fear. Do not look to the sides; it will distract you. Only look forward and keep walking with your heart surrendered to God in trust and faith, for He will surely bring you through and do what He said He will do. Remember His promises and align yourself forward as you walk to take hold of each one by faith.

Proverbs 4:25-27

Let your eyes look right on (with fixed purpose), and let your gaze be straight before you. Consider well the path of your feet, and let all your ways be established and ordered aright. Turn not aside to the right hand or the left; remove your foot from evil.

We are in a very intimate time with God at the moment. The walk has been very painful, but we have come to a place of rest as we lean into Him and trust…and wait. He is calling us to come up higher. I heard the Lord say, ‘I’ve come down into the valley to awaken, deliver, and heal you. I have put the spotlight on your heart as I’ve opened it up, layer by layer. Now, I’m taking you from the valley to the mountaintop, where I will show you My Kingdom and My purposes and plans for the earth. I am showing you things you haven’t known and things you haven’t seen, for only now are you able to understand. Only those who have suffered with Me will be able to go to the high places with Me, where I will shine the light on My heart and share its secrets with those I trust.

It’s the time of My Kingdom. Come, and let Me show You My agenda, My order, My system, and My provision. On the earth, there will be many storms in this time ahead. There will be storms in many directions, but I am calling you to look forward at My Word and My Promises, and keep walking and trusting. You will inherit, in spite of the storms that surround you; do not fear. The enemy wants to distract you on the left and the right; give him no attention. I have strengthened you through your difficulty and have changed your shape to cut through resistance and obstacles and to make a new way where there is none. Now is the time to be more resilient than ever, and to take hold of your faith like never before.

Heb. 12:12,13

So then, brace up and reinvigorate and set right your slackened and weakened and drooping hands and strengthen your feeble and palsied and tottering knees, and cut through and make firm and plain and smooth, straight paths for your feet (yes, make them safe and upright and happy paths that go in the right direction), so that the lame and halting (limbs) may not be put out of joint, but rather may be cured.
