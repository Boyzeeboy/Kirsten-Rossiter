God sets the scene for a miracle

6 September 2026

I’ve been reading the book of Exodus in The Passion Translation, and so many truths have been highlighted for me that we need during this time of stepping through the impossible into the promise.

1. Our detour is for our protection

Exodus 13:17 When Pharaoh finally let the people go, God did not guide them by the coastal road that went through the Philistine’s territory, even though it was the shortest route. For God thought, “If their enemies attack them, they will change their minds when they see war and return to Egypt.” Instead, God led the people by a longer, roundabout detour through the desert toward the Sea of Reeds. And so the Israelites marched out of Egypt fully armed in military formation.

God protected the Israelites from facing warfare that they were not ready for. Turning around and running back to Egypt would have been too easy if it was in close proximity. God led them further away from Egypt and further away from their promised land, along a roundabout way because it was in their best interest, in the bigger picture that they couldn’t see.

2. God strategised a plan to fool the enemy

Exodus 14:2-4 “Tell the Israelites to turn back and make camp in front of Pi Hahiroth, between Migdol and the sea, facing Baal Zephon. You must pitch your camp opposite this place, beside the sea. When Pharaoh hears that you have turned back, he will think, ‘The Israelites are wandering around confused. Now they’re trapped in the desert.’ Then once again, I will harden Pharaoh’s heart, and he will chase after you. I will use Pharaoh and his whole army to magnify my glory. And then the Egyptians will know that I am Yahweh.” So the Israelites followed God’s instructions.

Trusting in the Lord with all our hearts and not leaning on our own understanding allows Him to strategically use our circumstances to fool the enemy, prompting him to advance God’s plans. God sees the whole picture; we only see in part. Let’s lean into Him at this time when He is working our circumstances for His glory.

3. Do not yield to fear

Exodus 14:10 As Pharaoh approached with his troops, the Israelites looked behind them – and there were the Egyptians closing in on them. The Israelites were terrified and cried out to Yahweh for help.

Exodus 14:13 So Moses calmed the people and said, “Do not yield to fear. You have nothing to do but keep still and watch how Yahweh will save you today. The Egyptians you see today you will never see again. Yahweh will fight for you, so stay calm.”

When God sets the scene for His deliverance, the natural will shout intimidation at us as Goliath shouted at David. We need to fight against fear, for once yielded to it, we will only see the impossibilities and start complaining, grumbling, and fighting against the very thing God is trying to work in our favour. Keep still and watch what God will do.

5. Keep partnering with God

Exodus 14:15 Then Yahweh said to Moses, “Why do you keep crying out to me for help? Tell the Israelites to march forward. Hold out your rod over the water and split open the sea to make a path so that the Israelites can walk through it on dry ground between the walls of water on each side.

When we reject fear, we can rise above our circumstance and partner with Him. Our faith in action will open up the way for God to bring forth the creative miracles that He has destined for our situation.

6. God wants to display His glory

Exodus 14:17 And behold, I, the hardener of the hearts of the Egyptians, will make them so stubborn that they will follow the Israelites onto the seabed. And I will magnify my glory at the expense of Pharaoh and all his army, chariots, and horsemen. And when I have displayed my glory and defeated Pharaoh and all his army, the Egyptians will realize that I am Yahweh.”

Our deliverance is not only about us and God answering our prayers. It is also about us working with Him so that His desire is met: for the earth to witness His glory and be saved. When we say, ‘Lord, I give you my life, you can have it all’, He takes us up on that offer and works His strategic plans for the nations into our destiny. We have given Him permission to partner with us for His Kingdom.

7. Our salvation is the enemy’s judgment

Exodus 14:25 Realizing what had happened, the Egyptians said, “Let’s get away from these people. Yahweh himself is fighting on their behalf on their side against us!”

Exodus 14:30,31 That day, Yahweh saved the people of Israel from the clutches of the Egyptians, and they saw all the Egyptians lying dead on the seashore. When the people witnessed Yahweh’s mighty display of power against the Egyptians, they stood in awe and wonder of God. The people put their faith in Yahweh and in Moses, his servant.

We have a loving God who fights for us. The enemy wants us to yield to fear so that we stop or hinder the process of His miracle working in our situation. He wants us to stand against God, instead of partnering with Him in full surrender, humility, trust, and obedience. Because when we do, we will witness His great power and protection, and we will know how deeply and unconditionally we are loved. Our salvation is the enemy’s judgment.

So, in this time when the pressure feels increased and our circumstances seem more impossible than ever, we need to stand still and watch how Yahweh will save us. We also need to partner with Him by faith, protecting our authority in Him, for we are the head and not the tail, above and not beneath. Above all, we need to focus on His desire and His will, not our own, for in that place, we will be able to see our circumstances as a scene set by Him to display His great power and glory for all to see. We have become part of His story that He is narrating on the earth today, for today is a day of miracles that will be recorded for all time.
