The swelling of the tsunami wave

18 June 2026

There are slight tremors in the earth right now. God is starting to bring a shaking, but right now, we are sensing its coming and only the initial shifts are being felt. It's like the smell of the rain before it comes, or the gathering of birds before a storm.

The Shaking of the Remnant

God's remnant have been through a shaking like they've never known. I am reminded of Jesus on the cross, and Noah building his ark. In both instances, to the onlookers, it appeared that they were out of touch with the rest of the world, and their lives at that moment were not esteemed nor desired. However, both were in the centre of the Father's Will. Both were part of a story of salvation and had to partner with the Father to bring His purposes about for the earth.

As in the Days of Noah

It isn't a small thing that the Word says the last days will be like the days of Noah. They were eating and drinking, getting married and celebrating until the rain came. Until the judgment came. They didn't perceive it, they didn't expect it, and they didn't prepare for it. I hear the sound of rain on the horizon... it is coming. I feel the ripples in the waves building... it is coming.

Get Ready for My Storm

God is saying, 'Get ready for My storm! Get ready for My rain! Get ready for My wave! The earth have been drunk with the love of self and the love of money, it has been intoxicated with lust. I am bringing My waters of judgment that will shake the earth like it has never been shaken before. There will not be one man who will escape the fury of My storm. Those who have puffed themselves up with greed will be brought low and those who have been humbly walking before Me, will be lifted up high. I am going to turn the tables on those who have worshipped money and ruled as gods. I am going to deal with the evil in the nations, and who can stand in the fire of My judgment?

But those who wait on Me, who trust in and cling to Me, will not be ashamed. The hour has changed, My beloved. Just as the hour came when the waters poured down, and the hour came that My beloved Son were crowned, so you will find yourself in a new hour where everything will change. What appeared to be your downfall and your calamity, will in fact be the door of hope and the parting of the waves that you've been trusting for.

Patient Endurance Brings Inheritance

Jesus had to be patient. In the midst of the mocking and ridicule, He had to wait on Me. Noah had to be patient. When others partied and mocked, he had to keep building. Patient endurance brings My faithful ones into inheritance. Know that I am faithful and I am a good Father who will bring you into rich fulfilment. However, your life is woven into My purposes for the earth right now, and I need you to keep walking, being led by My Spirit. This is the moment of download, revelation, and activation. You are about to be propelled into My purposes and I will establish you quickly.

An Ark for a New World

As Noah built an ark to save humanity, and bring them safely to a new world, so I am using My remnant to do the same. Noah had to move by faith, not knowing what rain was. You have to move by faith, not knowing what this approaching storm is. However, My promise is with you, and My presence is with you. This is not the time of your calamity, but the time of your salvation and the time of creation coming into its right alignment with Heaven. It's a time of alignment.

Complete Trust for the Coming Wave

Complete trust is required right now. Things are going to shift quickly and suddenly, the wave is going to swell fast. Those who walk by faith and hear My voice, will surf this wave to the new land. Those who are driven by fear, will be overtaken by this wave and there will be great loss. I have been training My remnant so that you will be able to ride this wave and provide for others. This is not a time to look left or right, to listen to this voice and that voice, but to keep your eyes on Me and your ears open to My voice. Align yourself with Truth and Truth-Seekers, and stay far away from those who want to burden, control, and manipulate you through their belief system and agendas.

Cut away clutter and noise so that you can sense where I am leading you and where I am going. You are safe with Me.

Matt. 24:37-39
