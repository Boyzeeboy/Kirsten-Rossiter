God is partnering with us

23 August 2026

There are few things as frustrating as trying to get your kids to be obedient and just trust you, so that what needs to be done runs effortlessly and smoothly. When you need to explain, convince, and push, the whole experience delays the process and causes a lot of friction and frustration. We see this in the dialogues between God and Moses in the early chapters of Exodus.

We are living in very important times where God is needing to partner with His people, who can hear His voice and simply work with Him in obedience and in step. In this time of the ‘new’, God is going to work fast and dynamically. He can’t give us all the explanations and facts at every turn. He just needs us to trust Him in the running.

As seen in the early chapters of Exodus, Moses was caught up with himself and his shortcomings. He was caught up with the fear of man. He was caught up with the natural. At every turn, he had a reason why he couldn’t be the man, nor be fit for the mission that God had called him to. We see the frustration that God was feeling, the growing frustration of dealing with a son who is hard-headed, stubborn, and unwilling. The point here to note is that Moses was not able to partner with God easily because of one thing - fear. What counters that stubborn defiance is humble, surrendered obedience, which is taught and learnt through the process of suffering and brokenness. God’s people have been prepared for a time of commissioning where He needs to partner with us without a continual struggle.

Many of God’s people have gone through so much suffering during this process of preparation that we have developed symptoms of PTSD. God is calling us to a place of rest and intimacy with Him, where He can heal us from the trauma we have suffered. He is bringing us to His quiet waters of refreshment, where He is ministering to us Himself. It is important that we give Him this time, so that we can be the partners He needs us to be, trusting and obeying Him at every turn. Not needing answers to every question we have, but knowing that He is needing a people who will be in step with Him as things move quickly on the earth from the ‘old’ to the ‘new’.

God is so kind. He does give us revelation when we ask, and He does care about us having understanding and insight into His workings. However, that is a continual process of seeking Him as we walk, rather than not budging until we are assured of the ‘whys’ and ‘hows’ before we move.

It is going to be more important than ever to live a life free from fear. We can’t discern what God is saying, nor can we discern the lies of the enemy when we allow fear into our lives. I heard the Lord say, ‘The enemy inflates reality into something bigger than it is. When you are walking in truth and faith, you will see the lie and ignore it. However, when you are walking in fear, you will be fooled by it, agree with it, and walk straight into the trap that is set for you. The nations will be shaken to the core, but the worldly narrative that will accompany that shaking will be one filled with lies.

The enemy knows that I am God and that I am shaking the nations to bring forth freedom and change, to cut the earth loose from the bondage the enemy has held it in. I am setting My people free to uproot and build the nations afresh. I am releasing My Bride in the nations to partner with Me in inheriting the nations for My glory and My purposes. I am hastening My return!

The enemy’s tactics are always the same. He grabs the glory and the shine for himself. He speaks lies and pretends to bring the shaking for his glory. For his reign. He tries to change times and seasons and speaks, ‘this is the end of the world’ into the ears of the undiscerning, the fearful, and the weary. Do not be fooled, and don’t come into agreement with what you see in the natural without discernment.’

‘Moses went back to YAHWEH and said, ‘Lord, why do you treat these people so harshly? Why did you even send me here? From the day I came to Pharaoh and spoke to him in Your Name, he has treated your people cruelly, and you have done absolutely nothing to rescue them.’ Ex. 5:22,23

Do not misread reality as a playbook set by the enemy. Keep alert, keep awake, and stay in the place of rest and intimacy with Me. Put your ears to My mouth, and listen for My Voice. Believe My Word, remain steadfastly rooted in Truth, and keep walking in the midst of the shaking. You will be led out into freedom, and you will prosper when calamity and famine comes. Judgment is coming to the wicked, salvation is coming to the righteous. Many will cling to the righteous and will be led into freedom with them, like the Egyptians who joined the Israelites at the exodus.

There will be a day that the shaking starts, and a day that the shaking ends. What feels like it will never end, because of the severity of it, will end. A new day will dawn, and a new era will be born. However, you, My people, will run and build in the midst of this shaking because you will not be overcome by it. I have prepared you for a time such as this, and you will push through the storm, partnering with My Spirit in the midst of the chaos, in the midst of the shaking. You will not stand still, nor will you be passively staring into the storm, asking the questions that Moses asked in Ex. 5. You will know that I am working My wonders and that I am setting My nations free, and you will be the deliverers that I have prepared for a time such as this.’
