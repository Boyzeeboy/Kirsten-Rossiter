Reflection 6 August 2026

The need to choose

Why would someone who has walked closely with God eventually fall away from Him? In Hebrews 6, we find a sobering warning about the danger of turning away from faith. Like a marriage, this falling away can happen gradually, through resentment, bitterness, and unforgiveness. Suffering tests our hearts and teaches us obedience, but how we respond matters. Will we allow pain and unanswered prayers to harden our hearts towards God, or will we continue to trust Him? Nothing is worth risking the love we share with Jesus.
