# Title shortening: the decision, 22 October 2026

**All 13 proposed shortenings were accepted and applied. The 8 below were left exactly as Kirsten wrote them.** This file is now a record, not a request.

Titles over 60 characters, across the 280 published posts: **21 before, 8 after.** The longest is now 66 characters, down from 84.

Changing a title did not change any URL: the slug is the filename, so every old link, sitemap entry and 301 from the old WordPress address still works. `sitemap.xml` and `_redirects` were untouched by this change.

## The 8 that were left alone

These are all between 61 and 66 characters. In each case the sentence is doing something a shorter version would break. They are listed here so nobody shortens them later thinking they were simply missed.

### God's Throne will be Established through Righteousness and Justice

`blog/posts/gods-throne-will-be-established-through-righteousness-and-justice.md` — **66 characters** (85 with suffix)

This is a statement of the doctrine the post is built on, straight out of Psalm 89. Cutting either 'Righteousness' or 'Justice' changes what it says.

### Embrace the Bends of Change, for they will Accelerate your Ride!

`blog/posts/embrace-the-bends-of-change-for-they-will-accelerate-your-ride.md` — **64 characters** (83 with suffix)

The toboggan is the whole post, and the title only works because it has both halves: lean in, and go faster for it.

### Pentecost Fresh Fire will cause walls to fall and walls to rise!

`blog/posts/pentecost-fresh-fire-will-cause-walls-to-fall-and-walls-to-rise.md` — **64 characters** (83 with suffix)

The fall/rise parallel is the point. Dropping either side leaves a title that says half of what happened.

### God-Enthroned Speaks to Rebel-Kings, Laughing at their Schemes!

`blog/posts/god-enthroned-speaks-to-rebel-kings-laughing-at-their-schemes.md` — **63 characters** (82 with suffix)

'God-Enthroned' and 'Rebel-Kings' are your compounds from Psalm 2, and the laughing is the thing the post turns on.

### Heaven's Curtains are Opened, Tip over the Oil, Catch the Fire!

`blog/posts/heavens-curtains-are-opened-tip-over-the-oil-catch-the-fire.md` — **63 characters** (82 with suffix)

Three images in the order the post gives them. At 63 characters it is barely over, and the sequence is the title.

### The Book of Esther Parallels our Current Crisis in the Nations

`blog/posts/the-book-of-esther-parallels-our-current-crisis-in-the-nations.md` — **62 characters** (81 with suffix)

Plain, accurate and only two characters over. It also carries 'Esther', which is the search term for this one.

### We might be surprised, but God is never surprised by anything!

`blog/posts/we-might-be-surprised-but-god-is-never-surprised-by-anything.md` — **62 characters** (81 with suffix)

The contrast is the sentence. Any shortening leaves the first half without its answer.

### Three visions of the Lord to give understanding of our times.

`blog/posts/three-visions-of-the-lord-to-give-understanding-of-our-times.md` — **61 characters** (80 with suffix)

One character over, and it tells a reader exactly what is inside. Not worth touching.


## On the site suffix

Post pages no longer append ` — Kirsten Rossiter` to the browser title (#42). That suffix was 19 characters and Google counted it, which is why 92 of 280 posts were being truncated before it went. Between dropping it and accepting these 13, the number of posts whose `<title>` runs past 60 characters has gone from **92 to 8**.
