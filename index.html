The nations have been built from the top down. The Lord is building them from the ground up.

A new era is dawning for the church. As the old systems are shaken and exposed, the Lord is calling out a remnant—a prophetic-apostolic people who will pioneer His Kingdom models at grassroots level, where the enemy has no foothold.

Kirsten Rossiter's writing carries fresh revelation for the Bride of Christ: who she is, where she stands on history's timeline, and how she fulfils her mandate to govern in every sphere of society—from family and education to government, economy, media, and the arts.

This is not church as a building or a Sunday gathering. It's the Ekklesia, awakened to her authority, building what lasts.

About Kirsten

A prophetic
voice for
the nations.

Kirsten Rossiter is a recognised prophetic voice in South Africa. Operating with governmental authority, she unveils the purposes of God for the Bride of Christ in the area of governance across all spheres of society.

Qualified in graphic design and classical piano, an avid writer and visionary, she encourages others to combine their diverse gifts and skills to build the Kingdom of God wherever they are.

She challenges the way the church thinks—merging the spiritual with the natural, and the prophetic with the practical.

Passionate about global reformation and transformation in both the church and the marketplace, Kirsten is married to Warren Rossiter, and together they are raising their four children in Cape Town.
